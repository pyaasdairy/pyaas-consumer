Rules the app must follow

Who can buy what

Everyone: Parag (at MRP), ghee, wallet. Founding Family with an unlocked farm: PYAAS milk at the level 3 price. Everyone else sees PYAAS milk with "🔓 Unlock with Founding Family".

Prices

Always read from the ERP. Level 1 is MRP. Level 3 is the Founding Family price. Never apply a member discount to Parag.

Words

Founding Family, never Plus or VIP. "1 Litre Free Parag Milk", never "1 litre free milk". No priority slots, no "never auto-renews".

Offer limits

New customers only · one per mobile and address · select Lucknow areas · Android only · subscription needed · ₹500 top-up within 7 days for step 2 · T&C Apply.
