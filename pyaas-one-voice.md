# Part 6. How to keep one voice

- **One source of truth:** this file, `pyaas-one-voice.md`. Delete the older split files so nobody works from them. Anyone writing a page, a post or a deck copies the wording from Part 1.
- **Before publishing anything,** check the five numbers: farms, litres, cut-off, delivery charge, support hours.
- **When a fact changes,** change it here first, then on the site the same day.
- **Never publish a number you cannot show a record for.**
